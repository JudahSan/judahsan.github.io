## Synopsis

Duhigg explores the science behind habit formation and how understanding the habit loop can transform lives and organizations. The book reveals how habits work through a three-step process: cue, routine, and reward. Through compelling stories from individuals, companies, and social movements, it demonstrates how identifying and changing key habits can lead to remarkable transformations in personal productivity, health, and success.
