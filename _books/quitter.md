## Synopsis

Acuff provides a practical roadmap for transitioning from a day job to pursuing your dream career. The book challenges the conventional wisdom of "follow your passion" and instead offers a strategic approach to building your dream job while maintaining financial stability. Through personal anecdotes and actionable advice, it shows how to develop your passion, build a platform, and make the leap when the time is right.
