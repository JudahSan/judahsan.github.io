## Into the Void 🚀

**Into the Void** is a retro-inspired casual space shooter I created in 2021 as part of **GameUp Africa**.  
Developed with the **Unity Engine**, it was my entry point into game development and an experiment in building a simple but engaging arcade-style experience.

The game challenges players to **dodge asteroids, face enemy ships, and survive as long as possible** while racking up points.  
It’s light, fun, and built for quick play sessions on mobile devices.

👉 Try it on Google Play:  
[**Play Into the Void**](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gameupafrica.IntoTheVoid&pcampaignid=web_share)

---

### Current State

Into the Void captures the **retro charm** of classic shooters, but it’s still in a **rough and unpolished state**.  
As a first project, the focus was on learning and experimenting rather than building a fully refined game.

That said, I plan to **release updates in the future** to improve gameplay, visuals, and overall polish.

---

### Gameplay Highlights

- 🌌 **Retro-inspired design:** simple mechanics and nostalgic feel.
- 🎮 **Unity-powered:** built entirely in Unity as a learning project.
- 🛰️ **Pick-up-and-play:** designed for short, casual play sessions.
- 📱 **Mobile-first:** available on Android via Google Play.
