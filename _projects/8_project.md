[Admanus Group](https://admanusgroup.com) is a collective of founders in **agritech**, spanning **Kenya, Rwanda, Ghana, Nigeria, the UK, and the Caribbean**.

**My Role:**  
I contributed to the **development and maintenance** of the Admanus website, focusing on:

- Supporting updates, branding, and ongoing improvements

**Tech Stack:**

- **Styling & Interactivity:** CSS, JavaScript
- **Hosting:** GitHub Pages
