### TMS Companion App

**Project Purpose:**  
The TMS Companion App is designed to make memorization and study in the Topical Memorization Series (TMS) easier, accessible, and portable. Whether you're on **mobile, PC, or tablet**, the app ensures you can stay consistent in your journey anytime and anywhere.

Built as a **Progressive Web App (PWA)**, TMS Companion can be installed directly from the browser — no app store required — while still offering a native-like experience with offline support and home screen installation.

**Key Features:**

- Topical Memorization Series (A–E) with mini games
- Lyrics Catalog for plenary and group activities
- Plenary Guides & Illustrations
- Journal
- GRID Series verses from the GRID books with mini games
- Multi-language support (Kenyan dialects + international)

**Mobile:**  
TMS Companion runs seamlessly on mobile, tablet, and desktop — installable from the browser and fully responsive.
